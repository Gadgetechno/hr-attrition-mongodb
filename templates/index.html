Pipeline 1 – By Department

$group · $lookup

Groups employees by department, computes attrition rate, then enriches with $lookup to join the departments collection.

Pipeline 2 – By Job Level

$group · $sort

Attrition rate and average income broken down by job level (1–5) with a final $sort.

Pipeline 3 – Overtime Impact

$addFields · $cond

Does overtime drive attrition? Groups on the boolean field and uses $addFields + $cond to add a readable label.

Pipeline 4 – Income Distribution

$bucket

Fixed-boundary salary buckets using $bucket. Shows how attrition correlates with income ranges.

Pipeline 5 – $facet Dashboard (5 sub-pipelines in ONE query)

$facet

A single aggregation returns five completely different result shapes simultaneously — totals, by-department, income stats, travel impact and top-risk roles — in one MongoDB round trip.

Pipeline 6 – Engagement by Quarter

$unwind · $group

Each employee document contains an embedded engagement_history array. $unwind flattens it so we can group by quarter.

Pipeline 7 – Top 3 Earners / Dept

$push · $slice

Sort then group with $push to accumulate all employees into arrays, then use $slice to keep only the top 3.

Pipeline 8 – Age Bands

$bucketAuto

Let MongoDB automatically determine 4 equal-frequency age buckets with $bucketAuto, a powerful alternative to manual boundaries.

Pipeline 9 – Exit Interview Text Search

$text · $group · $sort

Full-text search on the exit_interviews collection using a $text index. Group matching exits by department.
